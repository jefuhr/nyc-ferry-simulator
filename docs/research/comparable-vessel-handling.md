## Calibration gate


No turning-circle, crash-stop, acceleration, pivot-rate, mass, inertia, resistance, squat, windage or derivative band is endorsed here. Before adopting one, require either vessel-specific observations with loading/environment/control conditions or a labelled derivation specifying every estimated input, formula, uncertainty and applicability limit.
