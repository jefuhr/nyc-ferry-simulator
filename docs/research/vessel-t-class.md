# River T-Class: Verified Source Statements

Treat these as attributed publication values, not measurements of every vessel or a current certificate of inspection. Access date: 2026-10-22. See [conflicts](conflicts.md) before choosing model inputs.

## Published particulars

- **T01** — IC16019 lists length overall 26.0 m, waterline length 23.9 m, beam 8.0 m, depth 2.8 m and maximum draft 1.9 m. [ic16019](https://www.incatcrowther.com/ships/ferries/commuter/ic16019/) · confidence: high.
- **T02** — IC16019 describes an aluminium catamaran under USCG Subchapter T, with 150 passengers and 8 crew in its capacity fields. [ic16019](https://www.incatcrowther.com/ships/ferries/commuter/ic16019/) · confidence: high.
- **T03** — IC16019 specifies two Baudouin 6M26.3 P3 engines, each rated 803 bhp at 2100 rpm, and two five-blade fixed-pitch propellers. [ic16019](https://www.incatcrowther.com/ships/ferries/commuter/ic16019/) · confidence: high.
- **T04** — IC16019 gives a service speed of 25.0 knots. [ic16019](https://www.incatcrowther.com/ships/ferries/commuter/ic16019/) · confidence: high.
- **T05** — IC16019 lists fuel 5678 litres, fresh water 757 litres and sullage 1987 litres. [ic16019](https://www.incatcrowther.com/ships/ferries/commuter/ic16019/) · confidence: high.
- **T06** — The November 2016 WorkBoat article gives River dimensions of 85 ft 4 in by 26 ft 3 in by 8 ft 6 in, draft 3 ft 3 in and freeboard 5 ft 10 in. [workboat](https://www.workboat.com/shipbuilding/new-york-ferries-taking-shape) · confidence: medium.
- **T07** — WorkBoat gives River machinery as two Baudouin 6M26.3 engines at 815 hp and 2100 rpm each, ZF 2050 gears with 2.519:1 reduction, and 38-inch five-blade NiBrAl Michigan Wheel propellers. [workboat](https://www.workboat.com/shipbuilding/new-york-ferries-taking-shape) · confidence: medium.
- **T08** — WorkBoat reports 149 passengers, three crew, 750 gallons of River fuel and 200 gallons of water. [workboat](https://www.workboat.com/shipbuilding/new-york-ferries-taking-shape) · confidence: medium.
- **T09** — Professional Mariner reports Humphree Interceptor trim tabs on Owls Head and its River-class siblings. [pm](https://professionalmariner.com/new-nyc-ferries-get-up-and-go-to-meet-surging-demand/) · confidence: medium.

## Research boundary


Do not interpret the crew field as routine manning, infer a COI limit, double the older tank figure, assign a maximum speed, or resolve the horsepower discrepancy without further evidence. Keep displacement, rudder geometry, controls and propeller pitch open; use the [capture questions](crew-questions.md).
