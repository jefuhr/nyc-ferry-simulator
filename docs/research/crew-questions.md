# Questions for Permitted Reference Collection

These are research requests, not assertions about fleet equipment or operating procedure. Follow [the reference capture kit](../reference-capture-kit.md); obtain permission before any observation, avoid distracting crew, and keep confidential, internal, security-sensitive or personal material out of the repo.

## Vessel and configuration


- Which vessel, hull identifier, date and machinery configuration does this observation concern?
- Is there a public or operator-approved shareable source for its class, dimensions, maximum draft and propulsion installation?
- Can any repower or cooling conversion be documented by a public completion record?


## Controls and machinery


- What are the engine-control make/model, lever detents, neutral/interlock behavior and station-transfer procedure?
- What are the gearbox model/ratio and propeller diameter, pitch, blade area and rotation directions?
- Is the helm follow-up or non-follow-up, and what rudder angles and slew rates apply?
- Are interceptors installed on this vessel, and which public manual describes the actual modes?


## Handling observations


- Are public trial curves available for acceleration, steady turns, normal slowing, emergency stopping and split-engine pivots?
- For an authorized observation, can load, trim, wind, current, depth, initial speed, RPM, control sequence and timing be recorded?
- Can the measurements distinguish speed through water, speed over ground and heading?
- What evidence would support a displacement or inertia estimate without assuming another vessel’s values?


## Landing and world geometry


- Which hull surface contacts which berth surface, and where are the boarding connection and fenders?
- Are public drawings available for surveyed berth coordinates, orientation, dimensions and fender properties?
- What non-sensitive, operator-approved information can describe approach, holding and line use without disclosing internal procedures?
- Can observations identify date, landing, tide/current, wind, vessel configuration and measurement uncertainty?


## Suggested capture-kit additions


Add hull/configuration and public-source identifiers to each record; separate equipment identification from inferred behavior; preserve measured conditions and uncertainty. Do not promise that a brief crew interview will close every unknown, and do not perform maneuvering trials solely to fill this dossier.
