## Public rules

- **G01** — 33 CFR Table 161.12(c) assigns Channel 12 (156.600 MHz) to the East River north of 40°42.40′ N at Brooklyn Bridge to Throgs Neck Bridge, excluding the Harlem River; the Channel 14 (156.700 MHz) entry includes Upper New York Bay south of that boundary. [cfr161](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/chapter-I/subchapter-P/part-161) · confidence: high.
- **G02** — Section 161.21 governs automated reporting; significant sailing-plan deviations are addressed by § 161.18(d). [cfr161](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/chapter-I/subchapter-P/part-161) · confidence: high.
- **G03** — Section 161.16(c) covers vessels certificated for at least 50 passengers for hire when engaged in trade. [cfr161](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/chapter-I/subchapter-P/part-161) · confidence: high.
- **G04** — Under § 161.23, scheduled-route vessels are exempt from Position and Final Reports unless directed otherwise; they provide a Sailing Plan 5–15 minutes before navigating within the VMRS area and resume established reports or report as directed for schedule departures exceeding 15 minutes or changed limited operating areas. [cfr161](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/chapter-I/subchapter-P/part-161) · confidence: high.
- **G05** — Section 26.04(d) designates Channel 13 (156.65 MHz), with the specified § 26.03(e) exception; § 26.04(e) adds monitoring of the designated VTS frequency within a VTS area. [cfr26](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-26) · confidence: high.
- **G06** — 33 CFR § 83.06 requires safe speed permitting effective collision-avoidance action and stopping within a distance appropriate to conditions. [cfr83](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/chapter-I/subchapter-E/part-83) · confidence: high.
