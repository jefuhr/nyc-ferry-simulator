# Landing procedures (SYNTHETIC example)

Everything here is invented to show the format. No real landing was observed.

## Floating barge (L1 to L10)

- Touches: starboard bow first, then flat alongside.
- Approach: about 3 kn, slowing from about 8 lengths out, final angle about 20 degrees.
- Holding: starboard engine ahead at idle with the helm to port.
- Lines: one bow line, passed once alongside.
- Deckhand: hand signal for distance to the fender; verbal for "all fast".
- Ebb versus flood: on the ebb the approach angle opens up; on the flood the stern is set off.
- Tricky landings: none (synthetic).
- Fenders: SYNTHETIC "foam fenders on the barge face, D-rubber on the hull".

## Environment rules of thumb (W2 to W5)

- Current sets you toward the barge on the flood (synthetic).
- NOAA station differs from the slip (synthetic).
